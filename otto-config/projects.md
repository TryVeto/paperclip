# Projects

## Product

Everything required to make Veto work: buyer funding flow, seller proceeds, provider integrations, escrow review product, deployment, Render, backups, staging, secrets management, CI/CD. Paperclip setup work belongs here when it directly improves Veto execution.

## Growth

Everything required to get Veto used: Citizens Escrow pilot, field sales, partnerships, outreach, content, meetings, case studies, relationship follow-up. Content exists to create meetings and trust, not as its own workstream.
